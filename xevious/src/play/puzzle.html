Xevious puzzle Zoom:
Drag a piece. Ctrl-click (or Cmd-click) adds a piece to the last picked one's group, and a group drags as one; Ctrl-click a member to take it out. Shift-click picks several for one move. Lock pins the picked pieces (gold). Arrow keys move the picked pieces by a tile (8 px), with Shift by 1 px. Alt while dragging turns snapping off. Drag the empty space to pan; Ctrl or Cmd with the wheel zooms.
